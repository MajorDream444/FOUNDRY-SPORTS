# Obed — Foundry Sports OS field brief

Prepared 7 October 2026. Proposed discovery assignment; no outreach has been sent by ChatGPT.

## Your mission

Help Major learn how athlete opportunities become actual work. Secure introductions to people who coordinate assignments and people who authorize spending. Explore your strongest relationships first; no Qatar or Gulf access is assumed.

Foundry Sports OS is a proposed sports package within HAMAL + Foundry OS. It aims to support institutions, professionals and athletes together. Our concept uses fictional data. We are testing the workflow, not selling a proven system.

## First three introductions

1. A club commercial manager, agency operations lead or sponsor activation manager who handled an athlete assignment recently. Priority: accessible person with real operational responsibility.
2. An adult athlete or academy alumnus involved in that assignment, through a permitted introduction.
3. An experienced adviser/coach/mentor who provides judgment repeatedly, plus someone who buys or funds that guidance. Gérard is a possible discovery participant; his role, availability and current access remain OPEN. Coordinate his invitation through Prado/Major rather than assuming participation.

Then expand to academy student affairs/career staff, parents and non-football pathways. A parent interview can concern their experience without collecting a child's profile.

## Interview sequence

Ask for name, role, responsibility and the outcome they want. Then:

1. Walk me through your last athlete-linked assignment, from opportunity to accepted delivery.
2. Which people, tools and approval steps were involved? Who owned the final decision?
3. Where did work stall? What happened, how often, and how much staff time did it take?
4. What already works well enough that we should preserve it?
5. What does each participant need to see, and what must stay private?
6. Where did expert judgment change the outcome? Could a guide help, or was a live professional essential?
7. Who would approve a paid test, and what evidence would they need? Is there a procurement/security process?
8. Could you supply an authorized anonymized brief for a manual trial? Who must permit that?

After the walkthrough, show the concept. Ask them to explain their next action without coaching. Observe confusion. Ask what they would delete and which step adds value. Do not substitute “Would you use this?” for a recent real example.

## Capture after each conversation

Role and organization; permission to attribute; date; actual workflow; tools; frequency; documented or estimated burden; permissions owner; operational owner; budget owner (if known); incumbent fit; data boundaries; sample assignment availability; agreed next action. Tag quotes REPORTED. Tag your interpretation ASSUMPTION. Record stated interest separately from budget and signed commitments.

Get permission before recording. Use anonymized notes when attribution is not permitted. Do not ask for private contracts, contact books, banking, health records or youth profiles. Do not promise legal compliance, investment advice, tax savings, automatic approvals or equity.

## Proposed checkpoint

Aim for three substantive walkthroughs before expanding scope. Review progress with Major after the first three, or roughly ten days after starting, whichever is useful. No automatic STOP for slow scheduling.

- PASS: recurring burden, authorized assignment, named owner/reviewers and measurable baseline.
- HOLD: missing authority, evidence, assignment or access.
- REDIRECT: a different funded need or existing tool/integration is a better fit.
- STOP: forced rights surrender, concealed fees or unsupported guarantees are necessary to proceed.

A paid trial needs a budget owner and agreed terms. Concept praise alone is not validation.
